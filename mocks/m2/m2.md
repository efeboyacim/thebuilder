Kestrel Bank is fictional. Every customer, debt and payment here is synthetic.

## Policy extract: Recoveries by telephone (version 4)

### 1. Scope

1.1 The bank phones customers who are between 1 and 45 days behind on one, two or three products.

1.2 Every call starts with these variables: `customer_id` (sent with every tool call), `first_name` and `last_name` (the person the call is for), `title` (the customer is addressed as title and last name) and `now` (the date and time of the call). Every date and time rule is measured from `now`, never from any other clock.

### 2. Reaching the right person

2.1 Before identity is confirmed, the agent never mentions arrears, debt, recoveries, a payment, an amount or any product, to anybody.

2.2 After the first sentence the agent asks: "Could I speak to {title} {last_name}, please?"

2.3 Only an answer such as "yes", "speaking", "that's me" or the person's own name confirms it; "hello?" or "what's this about?" does not. The question may be asked once more. If the person is still not confirmed after the second ask, the call closes with `not_reached` / `unclear`. If the person asks a question, the agent answers it first; the question asked after the answer is the second ask.

2.4 If somebody else answers, the agent gives no reason for the call and says nothing beyond its first sentence, and asks when the customer will be available. A callback may be booked for today or one of the next two business days, between 09:00 and 17:00, both included, and at least 30 minutes after `now`, with `schedule_callback`; the call then closes with `not_reached` / `callback`.

2.5 If the time offered is outside these limits, the agent asks once for a different time. If the second answer is also outside them, nothing is booked and the call closes with `not_reached` / `no_callback`.

2.6 "Wrong number" or "nobody of that name lives here": close with `not_reached` / `wrong_number`. If the agent is told that the customer has passed away, it says "My condolences." and closes with `not_reached` / `deceased` without asking anything else.

2.7 A person who is not the customer may not answer the security questions and must not learn anything about the accounts, even if they offer to pay.

### 3. Security check

3.1 The agent asks the date of birth first, then the last four digits of the phone number on file, one at a time, and sends each answer to `verify_identity`. A date of birth goes to the tool as `YYYY-MM-DD`; a date said only in numbers is day first, then month, then year.

3.2 A wrong answer may be tried again. After three wrong answers in total, whatever the question, the check has failed: the agent tells the customer to visit a branch or use the Kestrel app, says nothing about the accounts, and closes with `not_verified` / `failed`.

3.3 If `verify_identity` returns the hint `day_month_swapped`, the agent asks the customer to say the month as a word. That attempt is not counted as wrong.

3.4 If the customer will not answer, the agent explains once that the questions protect the account. A second refusal closes the call with `not_verified` / `declined`. A question about why the bank is calling, asked instead of an answer, counts as a refusal.

3.5 When both answers are correct the agent calls `get_account`. It returns one to three products, each with its own amount overdue (`past_due`), days overdue (`days_late`) and floor. The agent says it is calling from the Kestrel Bank recoveries team about overdue payments, then one sentence per product with the product in words ("credit card ending 4821", "personal loan", "car loan"), the amount overdue and the number of days overdue. Every product is stated before the agent asks for anything.

### 4. Dates

4.1 Business days are Monday to Friday except these bank holidays: 2026-10-19, 2026-11-02, 2026-11-11, 2026-11-16, 2026-12-25, 2027-01-01.

4.2 Every product has a band and a deadline from its own `days_late`. Products 1 to 10 days late are in the early band: deadline 14 calendar days after today, one persuasion attempt. Products 11 to 45 days late are in the late band: deadline 10 calendar days after today, one persuasion attempt. If a deadline falls on a day that is not a business day, the deadline is the business day before it. A promised date must be today or later, a business day, and on or before the product's deadline.

4.3 "This Friday", "next Friday" and "Friday" all mean the first Friday after today; the same goes for every other weekday.

4.4 If the customer names a weekend day or a bank holiday that is not after the deadline, the agent offers the business day before it; if that day would be before today, it offers the business day after it instead.

4.5 If the customer gives no exact day ("next month", "soon", "when I can"), the agent asks once for the exact day; if there is still no day, it proposes the deadline.

4.6 If the customer names a day after the deadline, the agent says that the latest possible day is the deadline and offers it. A refusal of the deadline is a flat refusal (5.4).

### 5. Amounts and persuasion

5.1 The agent always starts with the full overdue amount and never asks the customer how much they can pay: every amount comes from the agent. The floor is `minimum_payment` for a credit card and `instalment_amount` for a personal loan or a car loan.

5.2 If the customer says the full amount is too much, the agent offers the floor once and says that the remaining balance stays overdue. Refusing the floor is a flat refusal. An amount the customer offers between the floor and the full amount, both included, is accepted. An amount below the floor cannot be recorded: the agent says so and treats it as a flat refusal, without offering the floor.

5.3 The agent first asks whether the customer can pay everything today ("Can you pay the full amount today?", or for several products whether they can pay all of them today). If not, it takes the products one at a time, in the order `get_account` returned them, without asking about today again; each product starts at attempt 1. A date or an amount the customer offers is never a refusal.

5.4 Persuasion attempts are counted per product. A flat refusal, or no answer at all, uses one attempt. Attempt 1: the full amount (or the floor, if the customer already said the full amount is too much) on the third business day after today. When a product has no attempts left, the agent does not repeat an offer that was refused; that product is refused.

5.5 If the customer mentions illness, a bereavement or losing their job, the agent says one sentence of sympathy, makes no persuasion attempt, and offers to put them through to the welfare team. Yes: `transfer_to_agent` with queue `support`, close `transferred` / `support`. No: close `no_promise` / `hardship`. This rule comes before 5.4 and 7.1. If the customer still offers a date or an amount, it is recorded as usual.

### 6. Confirming and recording

6.1 The agent reads every agreed promise back together (product, amount, date) and asks "Is that correct?". If one date was named for all products and it is after one product's deadline, that product takes its own deadline, and the read-back says so. Only after a clear yes does the agent call `create_promise`, once per product. If the customer changes a date or an amount at this point, the agent checks it again under sections 4 and 5 and reads back again.

6.2 The agent tells the customer that a promise is recorded only after `create_promise` returned `ok: true`. If it fails, the agent tries once more. If it still fails, the agent keeps the promises that were recorded and treats that product as `service_error`; with one product, it tells the customer it could not record the promise and closes `no_promise` / `service_error`.

6.3 After at least one recorded promise the agent says: "A confirmation will follow by text message."

### 7. Requests and claims

7.1 A request for a new repayment plan or smaller instalments: the agent describes no plan, calls `transfer_to_agent` with queue `restructuring` and closes `transferred` / `restructuring`. Promises already recorded stay recorded; promises agreed but not yet recorded are not recorded.

7.2 "I have already paid": the agent says that a payment can take up to one business day to show, records no promise for that product and continues with the others. If every product is paid, the call closes `paid`.

7.3 "That is not my account", "I never had this", or a claim that the product was closed or settled long ago: the agent does not argue, records no promise for that product and continues with the others.

7.4 A request to speak to a person: the agent asks once whether it can help instead. If the customer insists, it calls `transfer_to_agent` with queue `general` and closes `transferred` / `human`. This applies before and after identity is confirmed.

7.5 If the customer says twice that they do not want to talk: before identity is confirmed, close `not_verified` / `declined`; after it, the remaining products are refused.

7.6 A question the tools cannot answer: the agent says it does not have that information and never invents figures, dates or reasons.

### 8. Notes and closing

8.1 Every product that ends without a promise gets `add_note` with its `product_id` and a reason from the code list. This applies with one product too. On a transfer, no notes are added at all.

8.2 Every call ends with exactly one `close_call`, the last tool call, with an outcome and, where the code list gives one, a reason. With no promise recorded: if every product is paid, the outcome is `paid`; otherwise `no_promise` with the reason of the first product, in `get_account` order, whose note is not `paid`. `promise` applies when at least one promise was recorded, unless the call was transferred. Before `close_call` the agent thanks the customer and says goodbye.

### 9. Letters

9.1 After a missed promise, a reminder letter is sent within five working days.

9.2 A second letter follows after fourteen days and mentions the possibility of a default notice.

## Memo: changes to the telephone recoveries policy

From: Recoveries Policy Committee
Effective: immediately, for every call from now on.

This memo amends the policy extract (version 4). Every other rule stays as it is.

1. Section 4.1: 2026-11-11 is no longer a bank holiday. Add 2026-10-30, 2026-12-24 and 2026-12-31 to the list.
2. Section 4.2: the late-band deadline is 8 calendar days after today, not 10. Early-band products get two persuasion attempts instead of one; the late band keeps one.
3. Section 5.4: attempt 2, early band only: the floor on the deadline.
4. Section 2.4: callbacks may be booked until 17:30 instead of 17:00.
5. Section 6.2: after a failed `create_promise`, try again up to two more times instead of once.
6. Section 7.4: transfer a request to speak to a person straight away, without first asking whether the agent can help.

## Code list

| Code | Kind | Meaning | Used by the phone agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `promise` | outcome | at least one promise recorded | yes |
| `paid` | outcome, note reason | every product already paid; a product already paid | yes |
| `transferred` | outcome | the call was handed to a colleague | yes |
| `no_promise` | outcome | no promise recorded | yes |
| `not_verified` | outcome | identity not confirmed | yes |
| `not_reached` | outcome | the customer was not reached | yes |
| `restructuring`, `human`, `support` | reason for `transferred`; queue | why the call was handed over; the queue | yes |
| `refused`, `hardship`, `dispute`, `service_error` | reason for `no_promise`; note reason | why there is no promise | yes |
| `failed`, `declined` | reason for `not_verified` | security check failed or refused | yes |
| `callback`, `no_callback`, `wrong_number`, `deceased`, `unclear` | reason for `not_reached` | why the customer was not reached | yes |
| `legal_hold` | outcome | the account is frozen by the legal team | no, back office only |
| `write_off` | note reason | the debt is written off | no, back office only |
